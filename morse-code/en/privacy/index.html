Lighthouse Morse App Privacy Policy

For the iPhone app “Lighthouse Morse” (home screen name “Morse Log”) | Last updated: November 12, 2026

In short: this app does not collect any personal data. Your watch log stays on your own iPhone, and purchases are handled by Apple, so we never see your payment details.

What we don’t collect

  • No account is needed. We don’t ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • There are no analytics, ads or trackers, and your usage is never sent to us or any third party.
  • The app doesn’t use the microphone, camera, location or contacts.

Data stored on your iPhone

Your watch log (lamps lit, mastery for each character, frequent mix-ups, minutes on watch per day), sound and speed settings, language, reminder and vibration settings are stored only in the app. If iCloud Backup is on, this data is included in your iPhone backup kept by Apple; we cannot read it.

To delete your data, tap “Erase my progress” in the Settings tab at the bottom of the app, or delete the app.

Purchases

Buying and refunding the full version are handled by the Apple App Store. The app only asks Apple whether this device has bought the full version. It never receives your Apple ID, card or other payment details.

Daily watch reminder and vibration

Reminders are local notifications on your iPhone. You choose the time, it stays on your phone, and nothing goes through a server. Turn them off any time in the app’s Settings tab or in iPhone Settings → Notifications. Vibration Morse uses the iPhone’s haptics and needs no permissions.

Saving your progress card to Photos

When you choose “Save Image” in the share sheet, your iPhone asks whether the app may add to Photos. This permission only adds pictures; the app cannot see or read anything in your photo library. You can practice normally without allowing it.

External links

The app links to research papers, which open in Safari. Those sites have their own privacy policies. The Knitting Hiyori website uses cookies for analytics and ads; see the Knitting Hiyori privacy policy.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions are welcome through the Knitting Hiyori website.
